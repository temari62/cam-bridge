pphumanseg.onnx - PP-HumanSeg portrait segmentation model (OpenCV Zoo build)

Used by client/background.py for the "Virtual background" option in Send mode:
it separates the person from the room so the room can be blurred or replaced.
Runs on the CPU through OpenCV's DNN module; no extra Python packages needed.

Source:   https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/human_segmentation_pphumanseg

Output: 1x2x192x192 float32 probabilities, channel 1 = person
